Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Who controls the data

Clipey is a free desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux for recording, editing and publishing videos. No Clipey account is required. The controller of the personal data processed to operate the app, the clipey.com.br website and the services described here is Produtora MaxVision, Brazilian company registration (CNPJ) 38.386.434/0001-44, based in Diadema/SP, Brazil (“Clipey”, “we”).

  • Privacy and data subject requests: privacy@clipey.com.br
  • Data Protection Officer (DPO, “encarregado”): Max Müller, maxmuller@clipey.com.br
  • General contact: contato@clipey.com.br

2. What stays on your computer

Recordings, video files, projects and exports are created and saved locally, wherever you choose. Preferences, settings, operational data and credentials are kept in the app data directory defined by your operating system. Provider keys and OAuth tokens are stored locally with Electron safeStorage, which uses the operating system’s cryptographic protection when it is available. Security also depends on your device, your system account, backups and operating-system support; it is not a guarantee against every form of access.

We do not maintain a Clipey user account and do not automatically receive your recordings or projects. When you use a connected feature, the data it needs leaves your device as described below, over the internet, and the receiving service processes it under its own terms and policies.

3. Connected accounts and publishing

If you connect Google/YouTube, Meta (Facebook or Instagram) or TikTok, sign-in takes place in the platform’s official authorization flow. The app receives authorization tokens and stores them locally so it can perform the actions you request. It may retrieve identifiers and basic information about the account and the available destinations so you can pick a channel, Page or professional account. When you publish, the app sends the video and the fields you fill in or select, such as title, caption/description and visibility, to the platform you chose. A post only starts through an action of yours in the app.

Google/YouTube connection, lookup and publishing requests go directly to Google/YouTube services. For Meta and TikTok, the authorization code and the token exchange, Meta callback and TikTok revocation operations pass through Clipey’s authorization service at api.clipey.com.br before the app receives the result, because these platforms require an app credential that cannot ship inside the installer. To do this, the service receives the authorization code or the token to revoke and the technical request data (such as IP address, time and user agent), and returns the result to your computer. Current information about that service’s technical logs and retention periods is available on request at privacy@clipey.com.br.

4. Google and YouTube data

Clipey’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Clipey does not use data obtained from Google APIs for advertising, sale, profiling or training AI models, and uses it only for the connection, lookup and publishing features you request.

Clipey uses YouTube API Services. By using those features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the data is also processed under the Google Privacy Policy. Google tokens are stored on your device, as described in section 2. You can revoke Clipey’s access at any time in your Google Account security settings (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). Disconnecting the account in Clipey deletes the token from your device; revoking access does not delete videos already published on YouTube, which you remove on YouTube itself. See also Data deletion.

5. Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

When you connect Meta, the app retrieves the Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts you manage, with their identifiers and access tokens, so you can choose the destination. When you publish, it sends the video, title/caption and visibility you chose. You start every post, and Meta processes this data independently under its own terms and policies. Instructions to remove access and request data deletion are on the Data deletion page.

6. TikTok

Posting to TikTok uses the Content Posting API (Direct Post), with your authorization. Before posting, the app queries the creator information TikTok provides for your account. When you post, it sends the video, the title/caption, the privacy setting you chose and the identifiers needed, then checks the post status using the identifier TikTok returns. Availability depends on TikTok’s approval and the permissions it grants: until the app has been audited by TikTok, posts may be restricted to private visibility. TikTok processes this data under its Developer Terms and other policies.

7. AI, link import and other services

  • AI providers you configure: the content needed for the task you choose (text, audio, transcription, images, video or project instructions) is sent directly from the app to the selected provider, with your own key. Depending on version and configuration, providers may include OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Mistral, MiniMax, Pexels and Hugging Face. The provider sets its own use, retention, location and any international transfer; review its policy before sending content.
  • Link import: when you paste a public link, the app uses yt-dlp to request and download the media from the source site to your device. The source site receives the request.
  • Announcements and updates: the app contacts clipey.com.br for announcements and GitHub for releases and updates. These services receive the technical network data needed to answer the request, such as IP address, time, user agent and requested URL.
  • Models and components: when you enable the relevant feature, the app may download third-party models or components, and the feature screen identifies the service.
  • Local MCP server: available only when you enable and configure it, and it listens only on your own computer. An AI or automation client that you run and authorize locally can read or change projects and use the tools you enabled.

8. Diagnostics and security

Diagnostics are generated locally and only leave your computer if you export and share them. The app tries to remove or mask credentials and other sensitive data from the bundle, but automatic redaction can fail; review the file before sharing it. We use operating-system-protected storage for credentials when available and encrypted connections with compatible services. No measure removes every risk, and we do not claim independent certification or audit. To report a vulnerability, email security@clipey.com.br or use private vulnerability reporting on GitHub.

9. Website and browser storage

The website uses your browser’s localStorage to remember your light or dark theme preference. The value stays on your device until you or your browser remove it. The site code sets no cookies and includes no analytics or advertising tools. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes technical request data (such as IP address, time, user agent and URL) to deliver and protect the site, under its own policies.

10. Purposes, legal bases, recipients and transfers

We process local data to provide the features you request; credentials and account data to connect and publish when you ask; the content you choose to submit to perform an AI task; and minimal technical data to deliver the website, announcements and updates, keep things secure and answer requests. The legal bases, under the LGPD (Art. 7) and, where applicable, the GDPR (Art. 6), are performance of the service you request, consent to connect accounts and publish, compliance with legal obligations, and legitimate interests in security and operation. You can stop using an optional feature or revoke an authorization at any time, without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

Recipients are the platform you choose, Clipey’s authorization service for Meta and TikTok flows, the AI provider you configure, Cloudflare (website hosting), GitHub (releases) and the source sites of imports. These recipients may be located in other countries; such international transfers happen when needed to carry out what you requested (LGPD, Art. 33, IX). We do not sell personal data and do not use or share it for advertising. Information about locations, sub-processors and safeguards is available on request at privacy@clipey.com.br.

11. Retention and deletion

You control retention of the recordings, projects and exports you save. Tokens and settings remain on the device until you disconnect the account, remove the setting or erase the app’s local data; pending revocation requests may stay on the device until they are processed. Third-party services control retention of the data they receive. Technical logs of the authorization service and of website hosting are kept for as long as needed for security and to meet legal obligations. To delete a post, use the destination platform. See the step-by-step guide under Data deletion.

12. Your rights

Under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018, Art. 18) and other applicable laws, you may request confirmation that processing exists; access; correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data; anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data or data processed unlawfully; portability; deletion of data processed with consent; information about whom we share data with; information about the option not to consent and its consequences; withdrawal of consent; and objection to processing, where applicable. Data subjects covered by the GDPR also have the right to restriction of processing.

Send your request to privacy@clipey.com.br or to the DPO, Max Müller, at maxmuller@clipey.com.br. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity or that of someone acting on your behalf, and to protect others. Confirmation or access in simplified form is provided immediately, and a complete statement within 15 days (LGPD, Art. 19); for data subjects covered by the GDPR, we reply within one month, extendable by two further months where necessary. Some rights have legal limits, such as data that must be kept to meet a legal obligation. You may lodge a complaint with Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or, in the European Union, with the data protection authority of the country where you live, work or where the alleged infringement occurred.

If California law (CCPA/CPRA) applies to your case, you may request to know and access, delete and correct personal information, limit certain uses of sensitive information, and opt out of sale or sharing, which we do not do. We will not discriminate against you for exercising rights provided by law.

13. Children

Clipey is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and connected accounts must meet each platform’s minimum age. If you believe a child has given us data through a channel we operate, email privacy@clipey.com.br so we can assess it and remove what is under our control.

14. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect actual changes in the app or the website. The revised version and date will be posted on this page, material changes will be announced in the release notes and, where the law requires it, we will ask for renewed consent before any new use.
