Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026 · Last updated: October 16, 2026

1. Who controls the data

Clipey is a free desktop app for Windows, macOS and Linux for recording, editing and publishing videos. No Clipey account is required. The controller of the personal data processed to operate the app, the clipey.com.br website and the services described here is Produtora MaxVision, Brazilian company registration (CNPJ) 38.386.434/0001-44, based in São Paulo, Brazil (“Clipey”, “we”).

  • Privacy and data subject requests: privacy@clipey.com.br
  • Data Protection Officer (DPO, “encarregado”): Max Müller, maxmuller@clipey.com.br
  • General contact: contato@clipey.com.br

2. What stays on your computer

Recordings, video files, projects and exports are created and saved locally, wherever you choose. Preferences, settings, operational data and credentials are kept in the app data directory defined by your operating system. Provider keys and OAuth tokens are stored locally with Electron safeStorage, which uses the operating system’s cryptographic protection when it is available. Security also depends on your device, your system account, backups and operating-system support; it is not a guarantee against every form of access.

We do not maintain a Clipey user account and do not automatically receive your recordings or projects. When you use a connected feature, the data it needs leaves your device as described below, over the internet, and the receiving service processes it under its own terms and policies. Audio transcription runs locally on your device. Optional cloud AI features run directly from the app to the provider you choose, with your own API key (BYOK), under that provider’s policy, and only send content when you configure and use them; there are no credits in 1.15.

3. Connected accounts and publishing

If you connect Google/YouTube, Meta (Facebook or Instagram) or TikTok, sign-in takes place in the platform’s official authorization flow. The app receives authorization tokens and stores them locally so it can perform the actions you request. It may retrieve identifiers and basic information about the account and the available destinations so you can pick a channel, Page or professional account. When you publish, the app sends the video and the fields you fill in or select, such as title, caption/description and visibility, to the platform you chose. A post only starts through an action of yours in the app.

Google/YouTube connection, lookup, publishing and revocation requests go directly to Google/YouTube services. For Meta and TikTok, the authorization code and the token exchange, Meta callback and TikTok token refresh and revocation operations pass through Clipey’s authorization service at api.clipey.com.br before the app receives the result, because these platforms require an app credential that cannot ship inside the installer. To do this, the service receives the authorization code or the token to refresh or revoke and the technical request data (such as IP address, time and user agent), forwards the request to the platform and returns the result to your computer. The service also receives Meta’s app removal and data deletion request notices. It does not write codes, tokens or account data to any database or other storage of its own. The service runs on Cloudflare Workers, which generates technical request logs (Cloudflare Workers Logs) with the client IP address, user agent, timestamp and the path of the requested URL. URL query parameters (query string) are redacted before logging, so OAuth authorization codes are not stored. These logs are retained for up to 7 days and then deleted automatically. Videos and posts do not pass through this service: the app sends them directly to the platform.

4. Google and YouTube data

Clipey’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Clipey does not use data obtained from Google APIs for advertising, sale, profiling or training AI models, and uses it only for the connection, lookup and publishing features you request.

Scopes requested: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload, to upload the videos you publish, and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly, to identify your YouTube channel (name, identifier and custom URL). Google data and tokens stay on your device and are not sent to Clipey servers, so no one on our team reads them. We do not transfer this data to third parties, except to Google itself to perform the feature you requested, as legally required, or for security.

Clipey uses YouTube API Services. By using those features you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the data is also processed under the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Clipey’s access at any time in your Google Account security settings (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions). On disconnect, the app also asks Google to revoke the token and deletes the token from your device; if Google does not confirm the revocation, the token stays encrypted on your device only so the revocation can be retried. Revoking access does not delete videos already published on YouTube, which you remove on YouTube itself. See also Data deletion.

5. Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

When you connect Meta, the app retrieves the Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts you manage, with their identifiers and access tokens, so you can choose the destination. When you publish, it sends the video, title/caption and visibility you chose directly to Meta; the video is not hosted at any Clipey address. You start every post, and Meta processes this data independently under its own terms and the Meta Privacy Policy. Permissions requested: pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts, instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish and business_management, used to list the destinations you manage and publish to them. On disconnect, the app asks Meta to remove the permissions granted. Instructions to remove access and request data deletion are on the Data deletion page.

6. TikTok

Posting to TikTok uses the Content Posting API (Direct Post), with your authorization. Scopes requested: user.info.basic, to identify your account, and video.upload and video.publish, to upload and publish the videos you choose. Before posting, the app queries the creator information TikTok provides for your account. When you post, it sends the video, the title/caption, the privacy setting you chose and the identifiers needed, then checks the post status using the identifier TikTok returns. Availability depends on TikTok’s approval and the permissions it grants: until the app has been audited by TikTok, posts may be restricted to private visibility. TikTok processes this data under its Developer Terms, the TikTok Privacy Policy and other policies.

7. AI, link import and other services

  • Cloud AI features (optional): optional cloud AI features run directly from the app to the provider you choose, with your own API key (BYOK), under that provider’s policy; there are no credits in 1.15. The content needed for the task you choose (text, audio, transcription, images, video or project instructions) is sent directly from the app to the selected provider. Depending on version and configuration, providers may include OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Mistral, MiniMax, ElevenLabs, Alibaba Cloud (Qwen3), xAI, Pexels, Hugging Face or an OpenAI-compatible service you specify. The provider sets its own use, retention, location and any international transfer; review its policy before sending content.
  • Local transcription: transcription on this computer runs on your device. The speech recognition models are downloaded once from Hugging Face (huggingface.co), and the alignment models from Clipey’s releases on GitHub; after that, they stay on your computer.
  • Mobile and tablet companion: your phone or tablet connects to your own computer running Clipey, over your local network or your Tailscale network (a third-party service subject to its own policy). Pairing is done by scanning a QR code with a per-device token that you can revoke at any time on your computer. Recordings, project data and edits travel directly between your own devices and are not sent to Clipey or Produtora MaxVision servers. The Android app (an APK distributed by us) can record the phone screen with your per-session permission; on iPhone and iPad, access is via the web app (PWA).
  • Link import: Link import uses a third-party component and downloads media directly from the source site to your computer. You are responsible for importing only content you own or are authorized to use, respecting copyright and the terms of the source site. Produtora MaxVision does not host, store or distribute imported content. The request with the URL is sent directly from your computer to the source site; no data goes to Clipey servers.
  • Announcements and updates: the app contacts clipey.com.br for announcements and GitHub for releases and updates. These services receive the technical network data needed to answer the request, such as IP address, time, user agent and requested URL.
  • Other models and components: when you enable the relevant feature, the app may download other third-party models or components, and the feature screen identifies the service.
  • Local MCP server: available only when you enable and configure it, and it listens only on your own computer. An AI or automation client that you run and authorize locally can read or change projects and use the tools you enabled.

8. Diagnostics and security

Diagnostics are generated locally and only leave your computer if you export and share them. The app tries to remove or mask credentials and other sensitive data from the bundle, but automatic redaction can fail; review the file before sharing it. We use operating-system-protected storage for credentials when available and encrypted connections with compatible services. No measure removes every risk, and we do not claim independent certification or audit. To report a vulnerability, email security@clipey.com.br or use private vulnerability reporting on GitHub.

9. Website and browser storage

The website uses your browser’s localStorage to remember your light or dark theme preference. The value stays on your device until you or your browser remove it. The site code sets no cookies and includes no analytics or advertising tools. The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes technical request data (such as IP address, time, user agent and URL) to deliver and protect the site, under its own policies.

10. Purposes, legal bases, recipients and transfers

We process local data to provide the features you request; credentials and account data to connect and publish when you ask; the content you choose to submit to perform an AI task; and minimal technical data to deliver the website, announcements and updates, keep things secure and answer requests. The legal bases, under the LGPD (Art. 7) and, where applicable, the GDPR (Art. 6), are:

  • Local features, mobile companion and AI tasks: performance of the service you request (LGPD Art. 7 V; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Account connection (OAuth) and publishing: performance of the service you request (LGPD Art. 7 V; GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).
  • Diagnostics you decide to share: consent (LGPD Art. 7 I; GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), which you may withdraw at any time.
  • Technical logs of the website, the authorization service and updates: legitimate interests in security and operation (LGPD Art. 7 IX; GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Handling data subject requests: compliance with a legal obligation (LGPD Art. 7 II; GDPR Art. 6(1)(c)).

You can stop using an optional feature or revoke an authorization at any time, without affecting processing already lawfully carried out.

Recipients are the platform you choose, Clipey’s authorization service for Meta and TikTok flows, the AI provider you configure, Cloudflare (hosting of the website and the authorization service), GitHub (releases and models) and the source sites of imports. These recipients may be located in other countries. International transfers needed for a feature you request rely on LGPD Art. 33 IX. For Cloudflare, which runs the website and the authorization service on our behalf, the transfer relies on the contractual clauses of Cloudflare's data processing addendum (LGPD Art. 33 II). When you download releases and models from GitHub, you connect directly to GitHub, which processes that data under its own policy. If you are in the European Union, your data is processed by the controller in Brazil. We do not sell personal data and do not use or share it for advertising. Information about locations, sub-processors and safeguards is available on request at privacy@clipey.com.br.

11. Retention and deletion

You control retention of the recordings, projects and exports you save. Data from the mobile companion (recordings and pairing tokens) is stored exclusively on your own devices until you delete it or unpair the device. Tokens and settings remain on the device until you disconnect the account, remove the setting or erase the app’s local data; pending revocation requests may stay on the device until they are processed. Third-party services control retention of the data they receive. The authorization service at api.clipey.com.br stores no videos and keeps no codes or tokens after completing the exchange, refresh or revocation; technical request logs stay in Cloudflare Workers logs for the platform’s retention period (up to 7 days). Website hosting logs follow Cloudflare’s policies. To delete a post, use the destination platform. See the step-by-step guide under Data deletion.

12. Your rights

Under Brazil’s General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018, Art. 18) and other applicable laws, you may request confirmation that processing exists; access; correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data; anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary or excessive data or data processed unlawfully; portability; deletion of data processed with consent; information about whom we share data with; information about the option not to consent and its consequences; withdrawal of consent; and objection to processing, where applicable. Data subjects covered by the GDPR also have the right to erasure (Art. 17) and to restriction of processing. Exercising these rights is free.

Send your request to privacy@clipey.com.br or to the DPO, Max Müller, at maxmuller@clipey.com.br. We may ask for proportionate information to verify your identity or that of someone acting on your behalf, and to protect others. Confirmation or access in simplified form is provided immediately, and a complete statement within 15 days (LGPD, Art. 19); for data subjects covered by the GDPR, we reply within one month, extendable by two further months where necessary. Some rights have legal limits, such as data that must be kept to meet a legal obligation. You may lodge a complaint with Brazil’s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) or, in the European Union, with the data protection authority of the country where you live, work or where the alleged infringement occurred. You may also turn to consumer protection bodies.

If California law (CCPA/CPRA) applies to your case, you may request to know and access, delete and correct personal information, limit certain uses of sensitive information, and opt out of sale or sharing, which we do not do. We will not discriminate against you for exercising rights provided by law.

13. Children

Clipey is intended for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us data through a channel we operate, email privacy@clipey.com.br so we can assess it and remove what is under our control.

Users under 18 may use Clipey only with the assistance or representation of a parent or guardian, as set out in the Terms; their data stays on the device and is processed only for the features described in this policy. Connected accounts must meet each platform's minimum age.

14. Changes

We may update this policy to reflect actual changes in the app or the website. The revised version and date will be posted on this page, material changes will be announced in the app 30 days in advance and, where the law requires it, we will ask for renewed consent before any new use.
