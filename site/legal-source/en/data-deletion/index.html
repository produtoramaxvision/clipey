Data deletion instructions

Effective October 16, 2026 · Last updated: October 16, 2026

Clipey has no user account. Recordings, projects and credentials stay on your computer, but data may also exist elsewhere: on the platforms where you published, with the AI providers you used, and in technical logs of the authorization service and website hosting. This page explains how to delete each of them. The controller is Produtora MaxVision, CNPJ 38.386.434/0001-44, Diadema/SP, Brazil.

1. Disconnect accounts in Clipey

Open Clipey, go to Settings → Connected accounts and click Disconnect on each account. The account’s token is deleted from your computer. For TikTok, the app also asks TikTok to revoke the token through Clipey’s authorization service; if that cannot complete right away, the request may stay pending on your computer until it is processed.

2. Delete local data

Close Clipey and delete the Clipey folder inside your system’s application data directory. By default, that directory is:

  • Windows: %APPDATA%
  • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support
  • Linux: ~/.config (or $XDG_CONFIG_HOME, if set)

That folder holds settings, cache, credentials and other operational data. Back up anything you want to keep first. Uninstalling may leave the folder behind. Recordings, exports and projects you saved in other folders are not deleted automatically: delete them with your file manager.

3. Revoke access on each platform

  • Google / YouTube: open your Google Account security settings (https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions), select Clipey and remove access. This does not delete videos already published: remove them on YouTube.
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook → Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations → Clipey → Remove. Removing the app prevents new access but does not delete posts already made: delete them on Facebook or Instagram. To request deletion of data held by Meta, use Meta’s own tools.
  • TikTok: TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services → Clipey → Remove access (menu names may change). This does not delete videos already published: delete them on TikTok.

4. Data deletion instructions for Meta users

This page is Clipey’s data deletion instructions URL for Facebook and Instagram. To delete Meta-related data: (1) disconnect the account in Clipey (step 1); (2) remove Clipey under Business integrations (step 3); and (3) if you also want us to delete data linked to your Meta account that is under our control, such as authorization service logs, email privacy@clipey.com.br with the subject “Meta data deletion”. We will tell you the result by email.

5. Request to the controller

To request access, correction or deletion of personal data controlled by Produtora MaxVision, email privacy@clipey.com.br with the subject “Privacy and data deletion”, or contact the Data Protection Officer (DPO), Max Müller, at maxmuller@clipey.com.br. Name the platform or feature involved and send only what is needed to locate the request; we may ask for proportionate identity verification. We will check each system under our control, including the authorization service and website hosting, and report the result within the deadlines of applicable law. Records that must be kept for legal or security reasons may be retained for the limited period needed.

The request does not delete data held by Google, Meta, TikTok, AI providers, GitHub or Cloudflare; ask them directly where appropriate. See also the Privacy policy.
