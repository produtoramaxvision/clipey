3. What the app does

Clipey lets you record, edit, import media from public links, connect a mobile or tablet companion app, use local tools or AI providers you choose and, after a voluntary connection, publish videos to third-party services. Recording, editing and project files are local by default. With the mobile or tablet companion app, connection takes place directly with your computer over the local network or Tailscale (a third-party service), and data travels between your own devices without passing through Clipey servers. The Android app (an APK distributed by us) can record the screen with your permission each session, and iPhone/iPad access is via a web app (PWA). Publishing, link import, updates, models and AI involve network communications described in the Privacy policy. Publishing requires an action of yours: check the destination, content, caption and visibility before sending. On TikTok, until the app has been audited, posts may be restricted to private visibility.

Social Media Publishing & Account Integrations

Direct Social Publishing: Clipey enables you to export and publish video recordings directly to your connected YouTube, TikTok, Facebook Page, and Instagram accounts.

Authentication & Token Security: When you connect an account, authentication is performed via official OAuth authorization flows in your web browser.

  • Local Storage: Access and refresh tokens are encrypted on your computer using operating system encryption (safeStorage / DPAPI on Windows, Keychain on macOS, Secret Service on Linux). Tokens are stored locally on your device and are never stored on Produtora MaxVision servers.
  • Auth Broker (api.clipey.com.br): To protect confidential application credentials that cannot be embedded in open-source software, token exchange and refresh requests for TikTok and Meta are securely proxied through a stateless Cloudflare Worker broker at https://api.clipey.com.br. The broker does not store tokens, authorization codes, user profile data, or media files. Cloudflare Worker edge request logs (containing your IP address, timestamp, request method, and URL path, with all query strings and authorization codes redacted) are retained for up to 7 days strictly for network security and operational integrity.
  • Direct Uploads: Video files are streamed directly from your computer to the official upload servers of Google, TikTok, and Meta. Your video files never pass through Produtora MaxVision servers.

Google API Services & YouTube Limited Use: Clipey's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We request only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly (to display your connected channel handle) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload (to upload videos you explicitly choose to publish). Google user data is never used for advertising, market research, profiling, or training machine learning models. You also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

TikTok Direct Post & Policy Declarations: When posting to TikTok, you must configure your audience visibility, comment, duet, and stitch preferences. In accordance with TikTok's Content Sharing Guidelines:

  1. By publishing, you agree to TikTok's Music Usage Confirmation and Terms of Service.
  2. If you disclose your video as commercial or branded content, you agree to TikTok's Branded Content Policy. Branded content visibility cannot be set to private ("Only me").

Meta Publishing Requirements: Publishing to Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts requires that your Facebook account is a direct administrator of the managed Page.

Disconnection & Data Deletion: You can disconnect any publishing account at any time in Clipey under Settings → Accounts & publishing. Disconnecting immediately removes local tokens and triggers server-side revocation with the respective provider. If offline, the revocation intent is queued locally and retried automatically. You can also request data deletion or view instructions at https://clipey.com.br/en/data-deletion/ (or via Data deletion).

Scheduled Posts Disclaimer

Scheduled Publishing Disclaimer: Clipey provides a local scheduling queue (publish-queue.json) allowing you to schedule posts for future publication. Scheduled posting is executed entirely by your local desktop application.

Clipey does not operate a hosted cloud scheduling service. For a scheduled post to publish, your computer must remain turned on, connected to the internet, and the Clipey application or its system tray process must be actively running at the scheduled time. If your computer is turned off, in sleep mode, disconnected from the internet, or if Clipey is closed when a post is due, the post will not publish and will be marked as "Missed" upon application relaunch. Produtora MaxVision disclaims all liability for any damages, losses, or missed commercial deadlines resulting from failed or delayed scheduled posts.

Developer Interfaces (MCP & CLI)

Model Context Protocol (MCP) & CLI: Clipey provides developer tools, including command-line subcommands (clipey record, clipey export) and an embedded Model Context Protocol (MCP) server allowing external AI agents (such as Claude Code or Cursor) to inspect project structure and perform editing operations.

Security & User Responsibility:

  • The in-app MCP server binds strictly to the local loopback address (127.0.0.1), requires a locally generated authentication token stored in operating system safe storage, and requires you to enable the "Allow AI edits" toggle.
  • The stdio MCP server (clipey mcp) operates locally without network sockets, inheriting the permissions of your terminal session.
  • You are solely responsible for configuring external AI agent permissions, securing authentication tokens, and reviewing all automated changes made to your video projects by third-party agents.
