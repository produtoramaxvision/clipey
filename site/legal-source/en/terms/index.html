3. What the app does

Clipey lets you record, edit, import media from public links, connect a mobile or tablet companion app, use local tools or AI providers you choose and, after a voluntary connection, publish videos to third-party services. Recording, editing and project files are local by default. With the mobile or tablet companion, connection takes place directly with your computer over the local network or Tailscale (a third-party service), and data travels between your own devices without passing through Clipey servers. The Android app (an APK distributed by us) can record the screen with your permission each session, and iPhone/iPad access is via a web app (PWA). Publishing, link import, updates, models and AI involve network communications described in the Privacy policy. Publishing requires an action of yours: check the destination, content, caption and visibility before sending. On TikTok, until the app has been audited, posts may be restricted to private visibility.

The MCP server is local and optional. If you enable it, local clients you authorize can operate on the projects and features you enabled. You are responsible for choosing and securing those clients.
