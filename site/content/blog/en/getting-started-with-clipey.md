> **Applies to:** Clipey 1.15 for Windows.

To get started with recording and exporting in Clipey on Windows, launch the app to select your screen or application window, test your microphone input, and complete a brief sample recording. In the editor, trim unwanted pauses or retakes, then open Export to choose a platform preset with size estimates and automated stream verification. The entire capture and basic export flow runs locally on your computer without cloud account sign-in.

## Install and open the app

1. Download the Windows version of Clipey from the official download page and run the installer.
2. Open Clipey from the Start menu. If Windows asks for screen-capture or microphone permission, review the prompt and allow only the devices you plan to use.
3. Close documents and windows that should not appear in the recording, then confirm which screen or window is selected.
4. Open **Device settings** to choose a microphone and camera if needed. Check the microphone meter and camera preview before recording.

Keep the first attempt simple and repeatable. That makes it easier to compare the recorded sound with what you expected to capture.

If you are recording a meeting, class, or demonstration with other people, let participants know and keep private information off screen. A short test can expose notifications, browser tabs, or apps you did not intend to include. Use the rehearsal to set framing and volume rather than trying to produce a finished edit on the first take.

## Make a short test recording

1. In the recording controls, choose an available capture source. Enable the microphone only if you need it.
2. Start recording and say a test sentence. Move the cursor over a control if you want to check how pointer movement appears in the recording.
3. Pause or stop when finished. Wait for Clipey to complete the file before closing the app.
4. Open the project in the editor and play the beginning, middle, and end. Check the picture, audio, and duration.

A test helps uncover problems early. A silent track can point to the wrong source or a missing permission; an unexpected window can mean you need to choose the capture source again.

If the capture appears frozen, confirm that the selected screen or window is still open and visible. Stop the test and make another short recording instead of relying on a file you have not checked. This also helps distinguish a capture issue from a problem that appears only during playback.

## Review and export

1. In the editor, remove only the sections you do not want and play the joins to check that they sound natural.
2. Open **Export**. Compare dimensions, frame rate, and estimated size with the intended destination.
3. Select a platform preset when it fits the use. Presets fill in settings that you can still adjust.
4. Choose **Export MP4** and wait for the result. Clipey runs technical checks on the file and shows a result or warning; a warning does not delete the export.
5. Play the file on your computer before sharing. For important work, review the whole video rather than relying only on the technical result.

## Tips

- Make another short test after changing the microphone, camera, or capture source.
- Use headphones while reviewing if the audio is noisy or the voice is difficult to distinguish from computer audio.
- Keep the original recording until you have checked the final export.
- If the computer has no compatible GPU, export can use the CPU and take longer.
- To prepare for publishing, organise the video and text in a [publish kit](/blog/en/video-cover-copy-publish-kit/).

## Limitations

Available sources and options depend on the devices installed and Windows permissions. Export verification checks technical file properties; it does not judge the edit or guarantee acceptance by a social network. AI features may depend on configured providers. Local recording and transcription do not mean every feature works offline.

For dimensions, estimates, and verification warnings, read [export presets and verification](/blog/en/reliable-export-presets-verification/). Check the [system requirements](/en/#requirements) too.

After reviewing the file on your computer, [download Clipey](/en/#download) and try a repeatable test recording.
